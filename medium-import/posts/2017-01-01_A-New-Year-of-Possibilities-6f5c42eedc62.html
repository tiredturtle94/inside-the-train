A New Year of Possibilities

2016 was a year of many firsts and many lasts, one of many historic feats and faults, but above all, 2016 was filled with lessons.

We all have our reasons to either love 2016 (for me, I graduated college!) or hate 2016 (world turmoil), but both can give us hope for the year ahead. 2017 will undoubtedly have its challenges, but people must rally behind the idea that there is always tomorrow, and as long as there is another breathing day, a new opportunity to change the world for the better.

I’ll leave you to your festivities, but keep hope in mind during 2017.

By Junior Gonzalez on January 1, 2017.
