We Are for Trump and Students for Hillary to debate next Tuesday

Representatives of We Are for Trump and Students for Hillary will debate next Tuesday, September 27, according to Onward State.

The debate, moderated by the speech and debate society, will occur at 8PM in 101 Thomas. It will take place a day after Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump debate for the first time at Hofstra University in Long Island, NY on Monday night.

Johnna Purcell (President of Students for Hillary) and Katierose Epstein (Students for Hillary Outreach Chair) will debate for Clinton, while Felippe Maher (VP of We Are for Trump) and Chris Baker (Communications Director for We Are for Trump) will debate on behalf of Trump.

Regarding Monday’s highly-anticipated first Presidential debate, We Are for Trump will not hold a debate viewing party as a group. Students for Hillary will hold a debate viewing party with the College Democrats in State College’s Democratic Campaign headquarters at 112 West Foster Ave starting at 8:30 p.m.
