Today’s the day

Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump have made their case to the American people. After hundreds of rallies, dozens of debates, and countless insults, it is your time to vote.

Today, The Bull-Moose Party will be in full campaign mode one last time, knocking on doors and calling supporters to make it out between 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. for their candidate.

They will not be holding a public viewing party as the results come in later tonight, but they do plan on meeting the day after the election, as they usually do, on Wednesday at 8 p.m. in the Willard Building.

Enjoy this historic day, and please, do your part in the democratic process and vote.

By Junior Gonzalez on November 8, 2016.
