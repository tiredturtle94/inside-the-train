Bull-Moose Party, Students for Hillary to debate tonight

The Bull-Moose Party and Students for Hillary will debate tonight for the last time before students and the rest of the country cast their ballots next Tuesday.

The debate will be held tonight at 8 p.m. at 10 Sparks Building, and will be moderated by the Penn State International Affairs and Debate Association. Debate topics will be foreign policy in relation to Iraq and Syria and Immigration.

Bull-Moose Party Chairman Robert Morss and Communications Director Chris Baker are debating for Trump, while PA Victory fellow and Students for Hillary member Ricardo Rojas will debate for Hillary Clinton. His other debate partner has not been confirmed.

By Junior Gonzalez on November 3, 2016.
