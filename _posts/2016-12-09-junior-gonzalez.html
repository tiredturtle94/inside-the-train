As a reporter in South Central Pennsylvania for the past four years, I have gained a wealth of experience in writing, online, print publishing and media management.

My storytelling career began in 2013 following my freshman year at Penn State University. I had my first internship at a non-profit mental health and rehabilitation organization managing their social media presence and writing compelling stories.

In my final semester at Penn State, I created my own website following students who supported Donald Trump during the 2016 presidential election. In the process, I and spoke with several members of congress as well as a presidential candidate, Jill Stein.

Since then, I’ve covered education, crime and transportation at several news organizations and currently write for LNP | LancasterOnline, the second-largest daily newspaper in the state.
