Updates: Monday, September 26, 2016

Good morning!

It’s debate day! No, not here, but at Hofstra!

Hillary Clinton and Donald Trump will face off on the same stage at the same time for the first time TONIGHT. It is expected to be one of the highest-rated programs of the year, and is expected to break records for political debates.

It also happens to be PSU Votes Week on campus. A special debate watch party will be held at the HUB-Robeson Center Monday night starting at 8:30 p.m. As previously reported, Students for Hillary will hold its own watch party in the State College Democratic HQ at 112 Foster Ave at 8:30 p.m. as well. The College Republicans will have a viewing party, but it is unclear where at the moment. They regularly meet on Monday nights.

We Are for Trump (which will not have a debate viewing party), will be tabling at the HUB all week this week, ramping up their efforts in these last six weeks of the campaign.

Stay on the train!
