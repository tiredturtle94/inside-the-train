The Bull-Moose Party — Photo Essay

For much of the Fall semester, Penn State students supporting Donald Trump for president worked on his behalf relentlessly. It was not without its share of setbacks, however, but the core group of students — about 20 in all — logged in hundreds of hours at the local GOP Victory office, and their hard work paid off in part with the president-elect’s stunning victory in Pennsylvania.
