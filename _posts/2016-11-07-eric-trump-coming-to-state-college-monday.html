Eric Trump coming to State College Monday

Eric Trump, son of Republican nominee Donald Trump, will visit the Republican Party Centre County Victory office in 232 S. Burrowes St. tomorrow afternoon.

According to the Centre Daily Times, Mr. Trump is expected to visit the office at 3 p.m. and speak with volunteers and visitors.
