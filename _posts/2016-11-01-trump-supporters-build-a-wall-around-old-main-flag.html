Trump Supporters Build a Wall Around Old Main Flag

The Bull-Moose Party (otherwise known as We Are for Trump) had their biggest demonstration yet in the most precious land at Penn State: Old Main.

The students for Trump created a makeshift hexagonal wall around the American flag at Old Main lawn and have been there since 11:30 a.m. Trump supporters clashed with Hillary Clinton supporters, who showed up around 1:30 p.m. to counter protest their demonstration, holding up signs, arguing with each other and even sitting in outside of the wall.
