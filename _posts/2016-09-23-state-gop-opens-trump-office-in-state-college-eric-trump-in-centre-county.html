State GOP Opens Trump Office in State College + Eric Trump in Centre County?

Good morning!

As you may have read, a Trump campaign office has finally opened in State College, with less than 50 days to go for election day. The office, located at 232 South Burrowes St., opened Wednesday evening, with Joyce Haas, vice chair of the Republican Party of Pennsylvania, as its inaugural speaker. Haas stayed to speak with student Trump supporters, asking them where they were from and engaging in simple pleasantries.

One big event this weekend could be at a Second Amendment rally in Howard township, where congressman Glenn Thompson will speak, along with a possible appearance by Eric Trump, the middle child of the Republican nominee. Attendants must pre-register, according to Thompson’s website.

Stay on the train!
